AI Act - regulatory framework | EU Digital Strategy

The AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024 and applies in phases.

High-risk AI system obligations apply from 2 August 2026. AI systems used in recruitment, worker management and task allocation are classified as high-risk.

Providers of high-risk systems must keep automatically generated logs for at least six months and allow human oversight of decisions.

Providers registered before August 2026 have until 2 August 2027 to bring systems already on the market into full compliance.
