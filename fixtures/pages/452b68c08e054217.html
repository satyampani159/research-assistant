Research: quantifying GitHub Copilot's impact on developer productivity and happiness

GitHub ran controlled experiments to measure how much Copilot changes developer output and how developers perceive that change.

In controlled studies, developers accepted just over 30% of Copilot's code suggestions, while perception of productivity doubled.

Perceived speedup was 55% while measured speedup was much smaller, a gap the authors attribute to the novelty of the interaction.

Developers who accepted more suggestions reported higher satisfaction scores, but the relationship was correlational.
