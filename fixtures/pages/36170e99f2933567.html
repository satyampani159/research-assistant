Developer Survey 2025: AI results | Stack Overflow Blog

The 2025 Developer Survey collected responses from 49,000 developers across 177 countries, the largest such survey we have run.

AI: 84% of professional developers are using or planning to use AI coding tools, up from 61% last year. Adoption is highest among developers who write code daily.

Professional developers reported using AI tools in 2025 at a rate of 84%, while students reported 92% usage.

Trust remains the limiting factor. Only 29% of professional developers trust the accuracy of AI tooling for complex tasks, and 46% actively distrust it.

Developers reported trusting AI suggestions for boilerplate far more than for security-sensitive changes.

The full results and methodology are published in the AI section of the survey.
