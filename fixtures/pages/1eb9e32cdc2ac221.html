Coding agents reshape developer tooling budgets

Tooling spend on coding agents grew to $1.2B annually, with most seats sold on annual contracts averaging $480 per developer per month.

Per-seat pricing across nine surveyed products ranged from $20 to $195 per month, a spread vendors attribute to differences in included model usage.

Several vendors moved to usage-based billing this year, and buyers report that prediction remains the hardest part of budgeting.
