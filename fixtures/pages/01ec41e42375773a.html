Enterprise adoption of coding agents rose to 38%, survey finds

A Reuters survey of 400 large employers found adoption of AI coding agents reached 38% this year, up from 21% a year earlier.

Spend is moving from experimental budgets to line items, and median annual commitment per adopter is $750,000.

Respondents cited code review capacity, not model quality, as the primary blocker to wider deployment.

Security teams reported that 17% of organisations had no policy governing agent-authored commits at the time of the survey.
